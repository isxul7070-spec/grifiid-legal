GriFiid

GriFiid is a free app for finding and listing rental homes in Canada. It lets people advertise a rental home and lets people looking for one find it and message the advertiser. It is not a landlord, a property manager or an agent, and it does not handle rent or deposits.

It is published by Abdiqadir Aden of Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada, and is available on Google Play as com.grifiid.app.

Want to delete your account? You can do it yourself in the app under Settings, and you can do it without the app if you have lost access. Both routes are explained here.

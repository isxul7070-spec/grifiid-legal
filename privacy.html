Last updated: 27 October 2026

How to reach us. Email isxul7070@gmail.com. We are the person to contact about anything in this policy, including a request to see or delete your information, or a complaint.

2. Why we are allowed to use your information

Under Canadian privacy law we rely on your consent. You give it when you create an account and agree to this policy, and again each time you choose to do something optional, such as uploading an ID or turning on notifications.

You can withdraw consent at any time by turning a feature off or by deleting your account (section 5). Some things cannot be undone — a message you have already sent may have been read, saved or screenshotted by the person you sent it to.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from Settings → Delete Account. There is no need to email us and no waiting period.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, your listings and their photos, your saved listings, your conversations and messages, and any verification request. This cannot be undone.

Your conversations go too, and that includes the other person's copy of them. If you only want a conversation out of your own inbox, delete the conversation rather than the account — that leaves their copy alone.

One thing survives, and we want to be straightforward about it:

  • Reports you filed about other people are kept without your name attached, so that a pattern of abuse does not disappear when an account is closed.

6. Keeping your information secure

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • The database enforces access rules row by row, so no ordinary account can read another user's private data, messages, saved listings or ID photos — this is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app. The one exception is moderation, described immediately below.
  • Chat photos, documents and voice notes are kept in a private area and are reached only through links that expire after an hour. A file you send can be opened by you and by people you are in a conversation with, and by nobody else. That has no exception: moderation cannot open them either.
  • ID photos and verification selfies sit in a private area no other user can reach, and never appear on a profile or go to another user. The only people who can open them are you and the person doing the review, and they are erased as soon as the request is decided — so once a decision is made there is nothing left to open.
  • We never see your password. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, which stores only a hashed version.

What moderation can see. When you report someone, we need to be able to look at what you are reporting. While a report you filed is open, we can read the messages exchanged between you and the person you reported, and nothing else: not their conversations with anyone else, not yours with anyone else, and not the photos, documents or voice notes in any conversation. The database enforces that limit itself. Once the report is resolved, that access ends automatically.

No service can promise perfect security. If a breach ever creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and equivalent provincial laws, you can:

  • See what we hold about you. Email us and we will send you a copy.
  • Correct it. Most details you can edit yourself in the app; email us for anything you cannot.
  • Delete it. Use Settings → Delete Account, or ask us.
  • Withdraw consent. Turn off notifications in Settings, or delete your account.
  • Complain. Email us first — we would like the chance to put it right. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca, or to your provincial privacy commissioner.

We answer requests within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity before we act, so that nobody else can request your information.

Delete your GriFiid account

Last updated: 27 October 2026

GriFiid is a free app for finding and listing rental homes in Canada, published by Abdiqadir Aden Yasin (Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada) as com.grifiid.app on Google Play.

This page explains how to delete your GriFiid account and the data attached to it. You do not need to sign in to read it, and you do not need to send anyone a request if you can still open the app.

The quickest way: delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open GriFiid and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings — the gear icon.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm twice. The second confirmation is Delete forever.

Deletion happens immediately. There is no waiting period, no review, and no need to contact us. Your session ends and the account is gone.

If you have lost access to your account — a forgotten password you cannot reset, a phone number or email you no longer use — email us and we will delete it for you:

We only ever use what you send us to locate the account and confirm the request is yours, and we delete the correspondence once the account is gone.

We action requests within 30 days. In practice it is much faster, and we will reply to confirm when it is done.

What deleting your account removes, and what survives

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from Settings → Delete Account. There is no need to email us and no waiting period.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, your listings and their photos, your saved listings, your conversations and messages, and any verification request. This cannot be undone.

Your conversations go too, and that includes the other person's copy of them. If you only want a conversation out of your own inbox, delete the conversation rather than the account — that leaves their copy alone.

One thing survives, and we want to be straightforward about it:

  • Reports you filed about other people are kept without your name attached, so that a pattern of abuse does not disappear when an account is closed.

How long anything takes to disappear completely

Deletion from the live database is immediate. Our database provider keeps routine backups for a short period, and deleted data disappears from those backups as they age out — we cannot reach into a backup to erase a single record, and nothing in an aged backup is used to restore a deleted account.

Verification ID and selfie photos are not affected by any of this, because they are already gone: they are erased as soon as a verification review finishes, approved or rejected, long before anyone asks to close an account.

Deleting something without deleting your whole account

You do not have to close your account to remove things from it.

  • A listing — open it from your profile and delete it. Its photos go with it.
  • A conversation — open it and delete it. It leaves your inbox; the other person keeps their copy until they delete it too.
  • Your verification photos — already erased once the review finished. There is nothing left to delete.
  • Your name, photo or other profile details — edit them in Settings at any time.
